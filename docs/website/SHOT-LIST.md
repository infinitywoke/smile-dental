# Photo shot list for the new website (20 Oct 2026)

Fifteen photos, taken in one morning at the clinic. Good photos are what make the new site look premium.

## The clinic
1. **The building from Shirva Road,** with the signboard readable (morning light).
2. **The entrance or staircase,** with the "1st Floor" sign, so patients recognise it.
3. **The waiting area:** tidy, all lights on.
4. **The treatment room, wide:** the whole room with the chair, empty and clean.
5. **The chair, closer,** with its light on.
6. **A neat instrument tray,** close up.
7. **Equipment:** the sterilizer and the X-ray unit.

## Details
13. **A teeth model** on the desk.
14. **Something local:** the view from the window, or Shirva Road.
15. **The clinic's logo** on a wall or door, if there is one.

## How to shoot
- **Light:** use daylight from the windows and turn on every light. Clear the clutter.
- **Camera:** use the phone's main camera at 1x (no zoom). Take each shot both upright and sideways.
- **Privacy:** no patients, no patient files, no screens showing patient details. Anyone recognisable must agree, in writing.
- **Sending:** send the originals through Google Drive, or as a "Document" on WhatsApp. Sending as a photo shrinks them.
